# Phase 2 Learning Check

Answer these after reviewing the generated Phase 2 outputs.

1. Why does an OSMnx road row represent a road edge rather than necessarily a unique road?
2. What is the difference between `highway` and the project's standardized road class?
3. Why is `building=yes` less informative than `building=apartments`?
4. Why are length and area calculated in `EPSG:2236` instead of `EPSG:4326`?
5. Why are raw and parsed fields both preserved?
6. Why should an unknown speed remain null rather than become zero?
7. What should happen when a source value cannot be standardized safely?
8. Why must invalid geometry be reported before it is repaired?
9. What makes a transformation reproducible?
10. How would these standardized 2D datasets support a later 3D workflow?
